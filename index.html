Our Story

Crafted for Quiet Moments & Warm Conversations

Founded with a passion for cozy aesthetics and artisanal flavors, Maison Xylia Café was born to be your home away from home. Named in honor of elegance and organic warmth, our café blends soft lavender tones, plush seating, soft ambient jazz, and the rich aroma of freshly pulled espresso.

Whether you're looking for a peaceful corner to read or study, catching up with dear friends over our viral Dubai Chewy Cookie, or enjoying an afternoon Matcha Latte, we ensure every detail brings sweet comfort to your day.
